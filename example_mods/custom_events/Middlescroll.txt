Enables/Disables middlescroll mid-song.
Disabling while it's already disabled does nothing.
List of on/off variables in the lua file.

Value 1: Enables/Disables Middlescroll 
(On=Enable, Off=Disable)
Leave blank for auto toggling

Value 2: Changes the Speed of the Transition
(Anything above 0 is fine)
Leave blank = 1 second
